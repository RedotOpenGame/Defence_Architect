From playtesting:
	
	So, the point of the game is really "amass a big army to defend your townhall".
	
	Another thing is that the player has the capacity to macro said units. Macroing is fun.
	
	What is not fun? Automated defence. Automatic turrets are making the game dull.
	
	So the idea is that the player would be playing two games:
		main is where they place buildings, controls units
		secondary is where the player would macro said units(to, say, get around the enemy and surround them)
